Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you should know before restoring, protecting, or maintaining your vehicle.

Most vehicles benefit from professional detailing every three to six months, depending on driving habits, parking conditions, weather exposure, pets, kids, and how clean you like to keep the vehicle.

Restorative detailing is a deeper cleaning and improvement process focused on safely bringing the interior, exterior, or full vehicle back to a cleaner, sharper, better-protected condition.

Restorative detailing can improve dirt buildup, road film, salt, light staining, pet hair, dust, grime, dull paint, light oxidation, and neglected interior or exterior surfaces. Permanent damage may require repair beyond detailing.

Light defects may be improved with appropriate polishing or paint correction. Deeper scratches, rock chips, and physical impact damage may require more extensive correction or body repair.

A typical restorative detail takes approximately three to four hours. Extremely dirty vehicles, excessive pet hair, significant staining, odor treatment, paint correction, or additional services may require more time.

Maintenance detailing is routine interior and exterior care for vehicles that have already been professionally cleaned or protected and need to stay consistently clean.

Many clients schedule maintenance detailing monthly or seasonally. The right cadence depends on mileage, parking, weather, pets, family use, and how quickly the vehicle collects buildup.

Maintenance detailing can include both interior and exterior care when requested. The service is built around the condition of the vehicle and the level of upkeep needed.
